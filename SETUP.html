📧 Email Configuration

Setup Gmail App Password for trainee confirmation emails

1 Enable 2-Step Verification

If not already enabled, go to Google Security and enable 2-Step Verification.

2 Create App Password

Go to App Passwords and create one:

  • Select app: Mail
  • Select device: Other (Custom)
  • Name it: Trainee Form
  • Click Create

3 Copy Your Password

You'll see a 16-character password (like abcd efgh ijkl mnop). Copy it!

⚠️ Important: Never share your App Password with anyone. It's different from your regular Gmail password.
✅ Ready to use! Configure in your HTML file.
Go to Registration Form
How to use: In index.html, replace the placeholder email settings with your Gmail App Password. The form will then send confirmation emails to trainees when they submit.
